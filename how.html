How to play

One egg a week. A new game every day. Play well, crack it open on Sunday, and see what hatches.

The week

Each day has its own game. Every game is worth up to 100 points, and every point you earn cracks the egg a little more.

  • Mon
    ScrambledSwap colored eggs into a hidden order. After every swap you see how many are in the right spot. The faster you sort them, the more points.
  • Tue
    Blind StopStop the clock at the target time. The clock disappears, so you have to feel it. The closer you stop, the more points.
  • Wed
    FlashSome eggs in the carton glow for half a second. Tap the ones you remember. Right picks earn points; wrong picks cost them.
  • Thu
    TriviaQuestions from this week's category. Find the right answer fast. Wrong taps add time.
  • Fri
    Link UpBuild a chain of five words, each one joining onto the last: SUN → LIGHT → HOUSE → BOAT. Solve each link fast. Hints help but add time.
  • Sat
    Pecking OrderPut five things in order, like oldest to newest. Anything in the right spot locks in green, so you can work out the rest. Fewer tries, more points.
  • Sun
    The Red EggOne round of every game, back to back. Then the egg cracks open.

Every round is rated

Each game has 5 rounds worth up to 20 points each. After every round you'll see how you did.

Hatching

On Sunday, your whole week decides what hatches. A strong week cracks the egg wide open. A rough one might only crack a little, and something may just peek out.

There are 15 chicks to collect, plus a few secret ones. Each stays a mystery until you hatch it. Every chick has a rarity, so you can tell at a glance who had the better week:

The only Mythic chick needs a perfect week.

Questions

When does a new egg start?

Every Monday. Sunday is always the hatch.

What if I miss a day?

You can go back and play any day you missed, as long as this week's egg hasn't hatched yet. You'll get the same puzzle everyone else played that day. You can't play ahead, though: each game unlocks on its own day.

Does everyone get the same games?

On the finished site, yes: everyone plays the same puzzles each day. During the beta, every play is random.

Where are my scores saved?

During the beta, in this browser on this device.
