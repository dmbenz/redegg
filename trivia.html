How to play

Each week has one trivia category. You get 5 questions with 4 choices each. The clock starts when the question appears.

The faster you find the right answer, the more points you get, up to 20. A wrong answer doesn't end the question: it's crossed out and adds 4 seconds, and you keep going. If your time plus penalties reaches 20 seconds, the question scores 0. Your 5 questions add up to a score out of 100.
