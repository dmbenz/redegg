The Red Egg

Flash

Round 1 of 5

Some eggs will glow for a moment. Remember where they were.
How to play

Tap Flash and some eggs in the carton glow for a moment, then go dark. Tap the eggs you remember glowing. You aren't told how many glowed, so pick as many as you think. Tap an egg again to unpick it, then press Submit.

There are 5 rounds. The carton grows and more eggs glow each round. The flash is half a second every time. Each round is worth up to 20 points: every egg you get right earns points and every wrong pick takes some away, for a score out of 100.
