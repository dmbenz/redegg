Each word joins the one before it to make a new word. The clock starts when you tap Start.
How to play

You get a starting word. Type the word that joins onto it to make a new word: SUN → FLOWER makes SUNFLOWER. Then that word is the start of the next link: FLOWER → POT, POT → HOLE, and so on, five links in all.

The first letter and the length of each word are shown. The clock runs the whole time, and each link is worth up to 20 points based on how fast you solve it, for a score out of 100. A hint fills in the next letter and adds 5 seconds, with a 5-second wait before the next hint. Skip gives 0 for that link and shows the word.
