How to play

Each round gives you five things to put in order, like oldest to newest or biggest to smallest. Drag items up and down to reorder them, then submit.

Anything in the right spot locks in green, so you can work out the rest from there. Get the whole order right in one try for 20 points; each extra try costs 4. You have five tries per round.
