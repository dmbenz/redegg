    How to play

    There's a hidden right order for the eggs. Tap one egg, then another, to swap them. After every swap you're told how many eggs are in the right spot, but not which ones. Use your past swaps to work it out.

    Round 1 has 3 eggs and each round adds one, up to 7. The clock starts on your first tap and stops when the order is right. Faster means more points. Your 5 rounds add up to a score out of 100.
