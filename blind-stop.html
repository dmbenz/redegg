How to play

Each round gives you a target time. Tap Start and the clock starts counting. It disappears partway through, so you have to feel the time. Tap Stop when you think you've hit the target.

There are 5 rounds. Early rounds show the clock for a moment before it hides; the last rounds are fully blind. The closer you stop to each target, the more points you get, for a score out of 100. You can also press the space bar.
